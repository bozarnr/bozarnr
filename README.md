# bozarnr

I am building a public, evidence-first AI quant research portfolio: small systems that make research claims auditable before they are allowed to sound impressive.

The theme across these repos is simple: generate ideas, test them with time-safe contracts, preserve negative results, and keep the claim boundary visible. The work is intentionally scoped as public research infrastructure, not private data or performance marketing.

## Current Focus

| Track | Repository | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| AI alpha research loop | [AI-Alpha-Research-Lab](https://github.com/bozarnr/AI-Alpha-Research-Lab) | Formula evaluation, promotion gates, rejection accounting, deterministic synthetic demo |
| Paper replication evidence | [Paper-Alpha-Replications](https://github.com/bozarnr/Paper-Alpha-Replications) | Five bounded replication cards: AutoAlpha, DoubleEnsemble, OpenFE, spatio-temporal representation, FactorMAD |
| Quant research tooling | [Quant-Research-Toolkit](https://github.com/bozarnr/Quant-Research-Toolkit) | Panel contracts, leakage checks, walk-forward splits, exposure neutralization, factor-family redundancy diagnostics |
| Behavioral agents | [Strategy-Game-Agents](https://github.com/bozarnr/Strategy-Game-Agents) | Repeated-choice simulations, baseline agents, participant trace validation, fitting, competition evidence ladder |

## What I Optimize For

- Auditable research loops: every promoted claim needs code, tests, and a stated ceiling.
- Negative evidence: rejected candidates are preserved as research signal instead of buried.
- Quant hygiene: point-in-time panels, leakage checks, costs, turnover, exposure controls, and redundancy checks.
- Agentic workflow discipline: agents can suggest and mutate candidates, but evidence gates decide what survives.

## How I Want The Work To Read

- Research systems over isolated notebooks.
- Rejection records over cherry-picked wins.
- Point-in-time contracts before metrics.
- Synthetic public demos instead of private or employer data.
- Small reusable tooling that can grow into larger AI-assisted quant workflows.

## Not Claims

These repositories do not contain private data, employer code, credentials, live trading systems, or deployable alpha claims. They are public research scaffolds, validation contracts, and reproducible demos.
